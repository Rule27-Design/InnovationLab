07

Questions, answered

The accordion animates open with native CSS — ::details-content plus interpolated height: auto. No JavaScript.

How fast can we start?
Signal sprints typically kick off within two weeks of a signed SOW. Retainer pods book one quarter ahead — the marquee up top shows current availability.
Do you work with in-house teams or replace them?
With, almost always. Our pods plug into your standups and your repo. The Embedded model exists for companies that want us to help build the in-house team itself.
Who owns the work?
You do — code, design files, brand assets, and the documentation to run them. We keep nothing hostage, including the hosting.
Can you handle regulated industries?
Yes. We've shipped HIPAA-aligned health platforms and SOC 2-scoped fintech. Compliance review is part of Shape, not a surprise during Ship.
What stack do you build on?
TypeScript end to end as the default — with the judgment to deviate when your context demands it. We choose boring infrastructure and spend the novelty budget on the experience layer, like this page does.
